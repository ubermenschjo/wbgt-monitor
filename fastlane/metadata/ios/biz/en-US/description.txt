A heat stroke prevention app for work sites that measures and records the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) in real time.

Check the WBGT at your current location at a glance with the semi-circular gauge. Records from start to end of work are automatically saved. CSV and PDF export is supported for site safety management.

【Main Features】
- Real-time WBGT display: Visual gauge showing the heat index at your current location
- 24-hour forecast: Check WBGT changes by time of day in advance
- Work recording: One-tap recording from work start to finish
- Danger level notifications: Get notified when thresholds are exceeded
- CSV/PDF export: Output as daily or monthly reports
- 7 work presets: Outdoor work, construction, farming, and more

【Danger Levels】
- Almost safe (~21℃): Green
- Caution (21-25℃): Yellow
- Alert (25-28℃): Orange
- Severe warning (28-31℃): Red
- Danger (31℃~): Dark red
