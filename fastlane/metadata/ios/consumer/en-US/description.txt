WBGT Alert helps you stay safe from heat stroke with real-time Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) monitoring.

See current heat index on an easy gauge, plan ahead with a 24-hour forecast, log outdoor activities, and get personalized heat alerts.

【Features】
- Real-time WBGT gauge at your location
- 24-hour forecast chart with recommended time slots
- Activity-based guidance (walking, running, shopping, etc.)
- Activity logs with CSV sharing
- Personal profile: age and health-based alert threshold adjustment
- Push notifications when heat exceeds your threshold
- Rainy season mode: indoor humidity and mold risk
- Home screen widget (iOS)

【For worksites】
For team records and CSV export on job sites, see our companion app "WBGT Recorder Pro".

【Disclaimer】
This app is not a medical device. WBGT values are estimates, not sensor-grade measurements. Consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.
